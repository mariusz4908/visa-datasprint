E-GROCERY GAP: Groceries are 26.3% of all Visa transactions but only 0.12% are online. In mature markets (UK, South Korea), e-grocery is 10-15% of food retail. Strategic Conclusions & Recommendations

1. Defend the Subscription Fortress

  • Apple, Netflix, Google, Spotify, Disney+, ChatGPT = ~8M tx locked on card rails
  • BLIK Recurring is a growing threat but still nascent
  • International platforms default to card-on-file — Visa's structural advantage
  • Action: Deepen partnerships with global platforms; offer better card-on-file UX; promote Visa tokenization for seamless recurring

2. Win the E-Grocery Revolution

  • Grocery = 26% of all card tx but only 0.12% is online
  • Online grocery avg is 402 vs 131 in-store (3.1x multiplier)
  • E-grocery at even 5% penetration = millions of high-value new online tx
  • Action: Partner with Frisco, Barbora, Allegro Grocery, Glovo to be preferred payment; offer cashback on e-grocery card payments

3. Counter BLIK in Domestic E-Commerce

  • BLIK: 67% and growing +5pp/year in e-commerce
  • Cards: 16% and declining -2pp/year
  • BLIK weakness: no cross-border, no chargeback, limited in-app
  • Action: Promote Visa Click to Pay as BLIK-like one-click UX; leverage Visa Secure for trust; target international shopping (AliExpress, Temu, Shein) where BLIK doesn't work

4. Capture the Service Economy Cash

  • Doctors (255K tx), dentists (76K), repairs (31K) = cash-heavy
  • 5-15% terminal coverage in tutoring, home repair, markets
  • Tap-to-Phone can turn any Android phone into a terminal
  • Action: Visa Tap-to-Phone rollout targeting: medical clinics, beauty salons, tradesmen, market vendors. Zero upfront cost, instant onboarding

5. Own the Micro-Payment Space

  • 65% of transactions under 10 PLN are still cash (NBP)
  • But in Visa data: 19.3M micro-transactions under 5 units exist (contactless enabled this)
  • Bakeries, parking, vending = high-frequency, low-value = habit formation
  • Action: "Tap for everything" campaign; zero merchant fees for under-10 PLN transactions; gamification of contactless micro-payments

6. E-Pharmacy & E-Health Opportunity

  • Pharmacy: 8.8M tx but only 0.18% online
  • E-prescription (e-recepta) is already universal in Poland
  • Telemedicine growing post-COVID (but payment is via transfer)
  • Action: Partner with e-pharmacy platforms (apteka.pl, WaptekaPL) and telemedicine (Telemedi, Docplanner) for card-on-file integration
